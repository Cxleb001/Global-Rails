Global Rails

Autonomous financial execution

Stablecoins to spendable local currency, in one conversation.

The problem

Getting stablecoins into money people can actually spend is still broken where it matters most. Someone in Kenya holding USDC has to find a DEX, manage gas, locate a separate cash-out service, and trust a quoted rate before any of it is spendable. Most crypto payment tools stop at the wallet and leave the last mile — actually landing in M-Pesa — as somebody else's problem.

The solution

Global Rails is a financial agent that does the whole journey in one request: check a live rate, swap on-chain, and pay out straight to M-Pesa — in plain language, not a multi-app workflow. It's built MCP-native, so the same swap, transfer and off-ramp tools behind our chat can be called directly by other AI agents and products, not only through our own interface.

Who it's for, and why it wins

Built first for Kenya's stablecoin-to-M-Pesa corridor: diaspora senders moving money to family, freelancers and contractors paid in stablecoins who need spendable KES, and fintechs or AI agents that want a working off-ramp as a callable tool rather than a project of their own. The edge isn't the idea — it's that the execution is real: on-chain settlement you can verify on Snowtrace, not a simulated demo, wrapped in an interface simple enough to use in one sentence.

Where we are

Stage – testnet, pre-launch
  • Working product live end-to-end — chat agent, on-chain swap, transfer, rate-check and x402 flows — frontend and backend both deployed.
  • Real on-chain swaps executing and verifiable on Avalanche Fuji (Snowtrace-confirmed), not simulated.
  • Live FX pricing feed for accurate USDC/KES quotes.
  • Safaricom Daraja (M-Pesa) payout integration built and in final sandbox testing ahead of production.
  • No live users or transaction volume yet — that's precisely what the ask below is for.

What we're looking for

Pre-seed funding to take the M-Pesa integration from sandbox to production and fund initial float; pilot partners — remittance platforms, diaspora-focused fintechs, mobile money aggregators — for a Kenya-first rollout; and early customers willing to move real value through it first.
